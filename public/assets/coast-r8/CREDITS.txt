Color maps: 2048 px WebP, sRGB.
Normal maps: 2048 px OpenGL normal, linear RGB. Alpha holds roughness.
Roughness is filtered at 256 px and quantized to 32 levels before packing.
Color and fine normal detail retain the photographic source resolution.
The rock geometry is procedurally shaped, not photogrammetry. The pale sandy
beach is an artistic interpretation requested for this scene, not a surveyed
representation of Nice's natural pebble beach.
